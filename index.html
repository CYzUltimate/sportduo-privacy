Privacy Policy — Sport Duo

Effective date: October 27, 2026
Developer: Sport Duo
Contact: lin84411@gmail.com

Sport Duo ("the App") is a sports picks and analysis app. This policy explains what information the App does — and does not — handle. The short version: the App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The App:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, name, or email address.
  • Does not collect personal information, usage analytics, or device identifiers.
  • Does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores one preference locally on your device only: your in-app language choice (English / 简体中文). This file:

  • Never leaves your device.
  • Is not linked to your identity.
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Subscriptions and payments

The App offers an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription ("Sport Duo Premium", $29.99/month). When subscriptions are enabled:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.
  • Google Play provides us with anonymous purchase status (e.g., whether a subscription is active) so the App can unlock premium content.
  • If a subscription-management service (such as RevenueCat) is used to validate purchases, it processes the purchase token provided by Google Play under its own privacy policy. No other personal data is sent to it by the App.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third party, because we do not collect any.

5. Children and age restriction

The App is intended for adults aged 19 and over (the legal gambling age in Ontario). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — of any age.

6. Responsible gambling

The App provides sports analysis for entertainment and reference purposes only. It does not accept bets or wagers, and nothing in the App is financial advice. If gambling stops being fun, help is available: ConnexOntario 1-866-531-2600 (Ontario). Please play responsibly.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App introduces features that collect data (for example, user accounts), this policy will be updated and the new version posted at the same URL before those features are released.
